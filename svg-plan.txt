svg_plan.py generates SVG plans of the kitchen (todo.txt) into plans/, one file per wall (left, top, right) and color set: kitchen_<wall>_colors<N>.svg. `make plans` regenerates them; `make plan-blend` builds kitchen_plan.blend (see the end). It also draws the whole apartment from the 3D scene, one floor plan per furniture layout (plans/apartment_layout<N>.svg, needs Blender); the layouts are defined in kitchen.py (build_layout) and are separate collections in the Blender files, layout 1 shown. Bedroom 1's closet with the library is its own collection ("Bedroom 1 Closet", layouts 1-5); layout 6 uses "No Closet" (a straight bedroom wall) instead.

Each plan has 3 views sharing the same horizontal axis, everything in cm:
- top view: seen from above, mostly the upper cabinets and the slab under them.
- front view: all door and drawer fronts with their color and ID.
- open view: doors opened 90 degrees, drawn edge-on over the cabinet frame on their hinge side (a flap lies over its bottom frame); drawer fronts removed, showing the drawer box (bottom and sides, 5 lower than the front); compartments are labelled with what they hold (Open, Oven, Microwave, 5 pull-out...), not with their shelf count; labels have a fixed size.
Elements are painted back to front by how far they reach into the room. Measures are shown on every view (cabinet widths and total, compartment heights, shelf lengths, depths in the top view), except implicit ones (ceiling, slab, plinth...).

Front IDs, e.g. KLB1d1a:
- K kitchen, L left plan
- B/T bottom/top (absent for column cabinets)
- cabinet number along the wall, in the described order (right to left)
- d for door or drawer
- stack index from the bottom, counting open compartments (absent if not stacked)
- a, b, c... from right to left, then bottom to top

Colors: wall cuts dark grey; wall and cabinet backs light grey; fronts from the color sets below (FRONT_PALETTE, COLOR_SETS; beige if unassigned); wood structure white; slab white stone; wall between slab and upper cabinets its own light grey. Labels override in CONTENTS.

Blender view (`blender -b --python svg_plan.py -- --blend kitchen_plan.blend`): the apartment of kitchen.py, except the kitchen, which is built from the three plans in 3D; the 30 x 70 corner section applies to the whole apartment. Plan-only parts (cuts, top-view footprints, parts drawn in two plans) are left out. Door and drawer fronts, with the door shelves that hang on them, are copied in one collection per combination of closed/open and color set ("Kitchen Fronts Closed colors1"...); open doors are at 90 degrees (KLB5da, under the table, only 80), the flap down; drawers stay in, their front removed to show the drawer box. Only the closed set 1 is shown by default. The walls outside the kitchen are not painted (all white). A curtain closes off the entrance and KL1: from the front corner of KL2's side, parallel to the top wall, then along KT1's left side to its front corner; it hangs from a rail at the ceiling down to 100 from the floor, in 10 wide grey bands (two greys). The entrance door is open 95 degrees; the plank table has a wood grain like the other wood parts.
